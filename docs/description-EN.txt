FULL DESCRIPTION (4,000 characters max)
--------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE APP DOES

During the time windows you set, every outgoing call is intercepted and replaced
by a pause screen: breathe, calm down, then decide. Not a wall. Not a punishment.
A few seconds between the impulse and the act.

It's 11 p.m. You know perfectly well you shouldn't call. You call anyway.
Instant Pause exists for that minute.

<b>Phone calls only:</b> WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram and Messenger calls are not
intercepted.

• You choose time windows (for example 10 p.m. - 7 a.m., every day).
• Any outgoing SIM call during a window opens the pause screen instead of dialling.
• A Zen mode can block calls on demand, for 2 hours.
• Three trusted contacts can always be reached: their call is automatically cut
  at 3 minutes.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

• The protection code is created cold, before anything else, and is never stored
  in clear text.
• Cutting a window, removing an allowed contact or turning off uninstall protection
  requires that code.
• No account. No ads. No analytics. No server.
• The app does not ask for permission to read your contacts: you pick one contact
  with Android's own picker, and only that entry is stored.

EMERGENCY NUMBERS ARE NEVER BLOCKED

On error or doubt, the call goes through. Always. Better to let a call through than
block an emergency.

PHONE CALLS ONLY

Instant Pause intercepts the calls you make with your SIM card and your phone's
dialler. Calls made inside WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Messenger or any other app
are not affected: Android gives no third-party app a way to intercept them before
they connect. If most of your impulsive calls go through those apps, this app will
not help you.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU

No network connection, no cloud backup. Windows, contacts and settings live in the
app's private storage, on your phone. The full policy is here:
https://ajanedouby.github.io/instant-pause-privacy/

SETUP IN 6 STEPS

1. Choose your code.
2. Turn on uninstall protection.
3. Allow the 3-minute hang-up.
4. Accept the call redirection role.
5. Allow drawing over calls.
6. Create a time window. That's it.
